Can't see what you want? Ask us.

There are two ways we can help with something that isn't on the site.

Made to your design

One of our partner manufacturers makes it to your size, colour and finish. Send a drawing, a photo or a description and we'll come back with a price.

Something we don't sell yet

If it's outside what our manufacturers make, we'll look for a reputable manufacturer in Egypt who can. This takes a little longer, usually .

What to send us

  • What it is and roughly how big
  • Where it will go, inside or outside
  • Photos or links you like
  • When you need it
  • Your rough budget

If you send a logo, character or design, you need to own it or have permission to use it. We don't copy other people's work.
