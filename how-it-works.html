How ordering works

There's no online checkout. You talk to us, we send a price, and nothing happens until you agree.

  1. Tell us what you'd likePick something from the site or describe your idea. A photo or a rough sketch helps.
  2. Get one full priceWithin we send a price that covers making, shipping, UK taxes and delivery. Nothing extra to pay when it arrives.
  3. We check it before it leavesWhen it's finished, our trusted colleagues in Egypt check it and send you photos and a video. Nothing ships until you're happy with it.
  4. It arrives at your doorPacked, insured and delivered, usually after you say go.

What the price includes

  • Making the piece
  • Checking it, with photos and a video for you
  • Packing and insurance
  • Shipping from Egypt
  • UK import VAT and duty
  • Delivery to your door

Not included unless we agree it

Installing the piece, carrying it upstairs, or taking the packaging away.

Paying

Nothing is paid on this website. Once you're happy with your price, we'll agree how and when you pay directly with you.

Questions? Just ask.

You'll get a reply from a real person, usually the same day.
