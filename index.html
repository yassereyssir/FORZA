Why we started

Egypt has manufacturers who are genuinely excellent at what they do, and very little of what they make reaches the UK.

Buying direct from abroad is hard. You can't see the work, shipping is a gamble, and import fees turn up on the doorstep. So we do that part. We speak to the manufacturers in Arabic, have every piece checked before it leaves, and give you one price that covers everything.
