Questions

What do you sell?

Products from trusted manufacturers in Egypt. Right now that's statues, furniture, décor and LED lighting, and we add more as we partner with new manufacturers.

How long does it take?

Usually from when you say go to when it arrives. We'll give you a date with your price.

Will I have to pay anything when it arrives?

No. Your price includes shipping, UK import VAT, duty and delivery.

How do I pay?

Nothing is paid on this website. Once you're happy with your price, we'll agree how and when you pay directly with you.

Can I see it before it ships?

Yes. Our trusted colleagues in Egypt check every finished piece and send you photos and a video. It only ships once you're happy.

What if it arrives damaged?

Everything is packed and insured. Send us photos within 48 hours of delivery and we'll repair, replace or refund it.

Can I return it?

Pieces made to your size, colour or design can't be returned unless they're faulty, because they're made just for you.

Can you find something you don't list?

Often, yes. Send us a custom request and we'll look for a maker. It usually takes to come back with a price.

Questions? Just ask.

You'll get a reply from a real person, usually the same day.
